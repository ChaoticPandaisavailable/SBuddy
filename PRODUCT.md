# SBuddy

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
完蛋！我被学习搭子包围了 / SBuddy is a personal campus study companion. Students collect schedules, manage courses and tasks, focus with pixel companions and develop independent relationships.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Seven surfaces: home, play, characters, daily activities, tools, gallery and settings. Three preset companions plus custom characters. Personal schedules and focus history are shared; relationship and appearance belong to each companion. Browser-local persistence, optional server-side AI, no accounts or cloud sync. Campus capture stays inside Tools → Schedule recognition. No background push promise when closed.

## Evidence
User supplied seven layout images and existing Vinext project. User approved warm campus style, three presets plus custom companions, six interactive rewards and direct implementation. Existing modules support animation, relationship rules, campus import, gesture recognition and AI endpoints. Multi-companion ownership and week calendar are new requirements.

## Principles
Preserve working capabilities. Never present demonstration records as user history. Imports require preview and confirmation. Keep independent companion memories isolated. Give usable local alternatives when AI is unavailable.
